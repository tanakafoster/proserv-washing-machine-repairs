# Content plan: blog articles

The plan started as 12 articles over 6 months, two a month, each mapped to a keyword that no other page targets (see `SEO-KEYWORD-MAP.md`). Nine articles are now written and live: articles 1 to 4, articles 8, 10 and 11 (published early, on 21 November 2026), the cleaning guide that took the place of article 5, and article 13, which wasn't in the original plan. Each article is about 1,500 words or more, in South African English, ends with a call to action and a list of sources, and links to at least one service page and one problem page.

Keyword research for the articles published on 21 November 2026 used Google South Africa autocomplete suggestions and Google Trends, because the keyword tools connected to the project couldn't return data at the time. See the note in `SEO-KEYWORD-MAP.md`, and check the keywords in Search Console once the pages have been live for a few weeks.

**How to publish a new article:** add a Markdown file to `src/content/blog/` (copy an existing one for the frontmatter). Draw its illustration as an SVG in `scripts/illustrations/`, add it to the `photos` list in `scripts/generate-images.mjs` and run `npm run images`, which renders any missing WebP into `public/images/`. Add the article's row to `SEO-KEYWORD-MAP.md`, then run `npm run build && npm run audit`, and deploy. The cards on the blog index, the sitemap and the "More from the blog" links update automatically; the written sections of the blog index (`src/pages/blog/index.astro`) don't, so add a short paragraph and a "Start here" line there too. Don't type the phone number into a post: write `{{PHONE}}` for the number and `[{{PHONE}}]({{TEL}})` for a call link (`{{WHATSAPP}}` gives the WhatsApp link). They are filled in from `src/data/site.ts`, in the frontmatter too. Use root-relative links with a trailing slash, e.g. `/problems/washing-machine-not-draining/`; the audit fails on links that don't resolve, missing `#anchors` and missing images.

## Rules for every article

- One primary keyword, used in the title, H1, first 100 words and meta description.
- No DIY instructions involving electrical work. Safe checks only (taps, hoses, filters, levelling, loads). If something trips the power, the advice is to leave the machine off and call a technician.
- Switch off at the wall only if the plug and socket are dry and you don't need to stand in water to reach them; otherwise at the DB board. Any advice to leave the door open mentions closing the laundry door if there are small children or pets.
- Prices in Rands, and only real prices (no invented figures).
- Back factual claims with the manufacturer's or detergent maker's own pages where possible, name the source in the text, and list the pages under a closing `## Sources` heading. Don't claim first-hand repair experience the business hasn't confirmed.
- 6 to 10 FAQs in the frontmatter, none repeating a question already used on another page.
- End with a call to action: phone, WhatsApp and the contact page.
- Link to at least one service page and one problem page with descriptive anchor text (never "click here").
- Add a real photo where possible (see README "Photos to take").
